# Script 3: The Click & Collect board

- **Length:** about 5 minutes
- **You need:** your phone, logged in to admin.technest.co.uk; a **test** Click & Collect order (place one on the website with a test card before recording, choosing Click & Collect at checkout). Never use a real customer's order.
- **Status:** Draft written from the brief - verify every step against the final UI before recording.

---

### Scene 1 (0:00-0:30) What the board is for

**On screen:** open the sidebar, tap **Click & Collect**. Show the three columns or tabs: **To pick**, **Ready**, **Collected**.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "This is the Click & Collect board. Customers who choose Click & Collect pay nothing for delivery and pick up the order here in the shop. Every order moves through three steps: To pick, Ready, and Collected. You can do all of it with one hand on your phone."

### Scene 2 (0:30-1:30) Read an order card

**On screen:** tap **To pick**. Point at one order card: the order number, the customer's name, the items, how long ago it was placed.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "To pick shows new orders that nobody has packed yet. The oldest is at the top, so start there. Each card shows the order number, the customer's name, the items, and how long ago they ordered. Read the items and pick them from the shelf. Check the colour and the model. A case for a 16 Pro is not a case for a 16."

### Scene 3 (1:30-2:30) Mark it Ready

**On screen:** put the items in a bag. Tap the button on the card to move it to **Ready**. Show the card now in the Ready column with its **collection code** (6 letters and numbers, like K7MQ2X).

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "When the bag is packed, tap the button to move it to Ready. Two things happen. The order gets a collection code, six letters and numbers. And the customer gets an email saying the order is ready, with the code, our address and today's opening hours. Write the order number and the name on the bag, and put it behind the counter. If you tap Ready twice by mistake, nothing bad happens. The customer only gets one email."

### Scene 4 (2:30-3:45) The customer arrives

**On screen:** tap **Ready**. Find the order. Show the collection code on the card. Tap the button to mark it **Collected**. Show the card move to **Collected**.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "Now the customer comes in. Ask for their name and their collection code. Find the order in Ready and check the code on the card matches. Only hand over the bag if it matches. Then tap Collected. This is important: the customer's card is only charged when you tap Collected. Until then, the money is only held on their card. So always tap Collected when the bag leaves the shop, not before, and never forget it."

### Scene 5 (3:45-4:30) Orders that are not collected

**On screen:** point at a Ready order that is a few days old.

**Say:** "Some people forget. Three days after an order is marked Ready, the customer gets a reminder email automatically. After seven days, if it's still not collected, the order is cancelled automatically and the hold on their card is released. They are not charged. Cancelled orders disappear from the board. Put the items back on the shelf."

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI (check whether the card shows that a reminder was sent)

### Scene 6 (4:30-5:00) Wrap up

**Say:** "So: To pick, pack it, Ready. Check the code at the counter, then Collected. Look at this board every morning, and again during the day."

---

## Common mistakes / if something goes wrong

- **Tapped Collected by mistake before the customer came**: the card was charged. Don't tap anything else. Tell the lead; a refund is done from the normal order page in the admin.
- **A message that starts "Payment captured but..."**: the payment went through, but a later step failed. **Nothing is refunded.** Tap Collected again. It finishes the rest without charging twice. Show the message to the lead if it keeps happening.
- **Collected is refused**: the order may not be marked Ready yet, it may be cancelled, or the card hold has already ended (for example after 7 days). Read the message. Do not hand over goods without payment; ask the customer to pay at the till or order again.
- **The code doesn't match**: don't hand over the bag. Check the name and order number, and ask for the email.
- **An order is missing from the board**: cancelled orders never show on the board. Check **Orders** in the sidebar.

## Rules to remember

- Payment is taken only when you tap **Collected**.
- Always check the collection code at the counter.
- Reminder email after 3 days in Ready. Auto-cancel and card hold released after 7 days.
- Click & Collect is always free. £1 add-on items on their own are fine for Click & Collect.
