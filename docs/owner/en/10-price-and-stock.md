# Script 10: Price & stock, change a price or a stock count

- **Length:** about 3 minutes
- **You need:** your phone logged in to admin.technest.co.uk and a test product (one from script 1 is fine).
- **Where:** menu → **Products** → **Price & stock** tab (address `/app/shop-products?tab=stock`). **Today** → "draft products to publish" opens the same tab with **Drafts only** on.
- **Status:** checked against the admin code on 2026-10-11.
- **Goal:** find a product, change its price or how many you have, press Save. Everything else about a product stays on its product page.

---

### Scene 1 (0:00-0:30) The tab

**Tap:** menu → **Products** → **Price & stock**.

**You'll see:** "Price & stock", "Find the product, change the price or how many you have, press Save.", a **Search by name or SKU** box, and two buttons: **All products** and **Drafts only**. Below: one card per product (one per colour or size when a product has several), newest first.

**Say:** "This is the quick way to change a price or a stock count without opening the product. Twenty newest are shown; I search for older ones."

### Scene 2 (0:30-1:30) Change a price

**Tap:** type part of the name or the SKU in **Search**. On the card, change **Price £ (inc. VAT)** to `5.49`. Tap **Save**.

**You'll see:** "USB-C cable saved", the button says **Saved**, and the card shows 5.49.

**Say:** "Prices are pounds and pence, VAT included: what the customer pays. The website shows the new price within a minute. Trade prices are separate and don't change here."

### Scene 3 (1:30-2:30) Change the stock

**Tap:** change **In stock** to the number on the shelf, say `12`. **Save**.

**You'll see:** "… saved" and the new number.

Two messages can stop a save, both on purpose:

- "The stock for this product is now 2, not 3. Reload the row and try again." You typed against an old number: an order was sent, or a colleague saved, since the page loaded. The card reloads by itself with the current number. Look at it, type the new count again, **Save**.
- "2 are set aside for orders that have not been sent yet …" Customers have already bought 2 of these. The stock can't go below that. Send or cancel those orders first.

**Say:** "If the number moved under my feet, the page tells me and shows the real number before it lets me save. That stops me from undoing a sale I didn't see."

### Scene 4 (2:30-3:00) Drafts, and the rest of the product

**Tap:** **Drafts only**.

**You'll see:** only products not on the website yet, each with an orange **Draft** badge.

**Tap:** **open the product** on a card to publish it, change the photo, name, colours or sizes. ⚠ (Medusa's product page: menu ⋯ → **Edit** → **Status** → **Published** → **Save**.)

---

## What can go wrong

| On the screen | What it means and what to do |
|---|---|
| "Price must be pounds and pence, like 4.99" | Use a dot, at most two decimals, no £ sign. |
| "Stock must be a whole number, like 12" | No decimals, no minus. |
| "The stock for this product is now …, not …. Reload the row and try again." | The number changed since you opened the page. The card reloads; type the count again. |
| "… are set aside for orders that have not been sent yet …" | Sold but not sent yet. Stock can't go below that number. |
| "This product's stock is not tracked here, so its stock can't be changed from this page." | The product does not count stock (set up that way on its product page). Its price still saves. |
| "… has more than one standard price …" | The product was given two retail prices on Medusa's page. Open the product and remove one. |
| "Nothing matches …" | Check the spelling, or search by SKU. |

## Rules to remember

- Prices include VAT and are in pounds.
- A stock count you type replaces the old one; it is not added to it.
- Stock set aside for unsent orders is not yours to lower.
- Publishing, photos, names and variants: the product page.
