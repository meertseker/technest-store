# Script 6: Trade applications and repair bookings

- **Length:** about 5 minutes
- **You need:** your phone, logged in to admin.technest.co.uk; two **test** trade applications (send them from the website's trade form with test accounts) and one **test** repair booking (send it from the website's repair form with your own phone number).
- **Status:** Draft written from the brief - verify every step against the final UI before recording.

---

## Part A: Trade applications (0:00-2:40)

### Scene 1 (0:00-0:40) What trade accounts are

**On screen:** sidebar, tap **Trade applications**. Show the list of **pending** applications.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "Businesses, like other repair shops, can apply for a trade account on the website. Once you approve them, they see trade prices without VAT, with lower prices for bigger quantities. New applications come here. You also get an email for each one."

### Scene 2 (0:40-1:30) Check an application

**On screen:** open one application. Point at: company name, business type, VAT number, Companies House number, contact name, phone and email.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "Open an application. You see the company name, the type of business, the VAT number and the Companies House number if they gave them, and a contact person. Check they are a real business. You can look up the company number on the Companies House website. If you know them, even better."

### Scene 3 (1:30-1:55) Approve

**On screen:** tap **Approve**. Show the status change to approved.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "If they're fine, tap Approve. One tap. They get an email to say they're approved. From now on they see trade prices, on every product that has trade prices set."

### Scene 4 (1:55-2:40) Reject, with a reason

**On screen:** open the second application. Tap **Reject**. Show that it asks for a reason. Type: "We could not confirm your business. Please apply again with your Companies House number." Confirm.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "If you can't approve them, tap Reject. You must write a reason. The customer gets it by email, so write it politely and clearly, and tell them what to do. They can apply again later."

## Part B: Repair bookings (2:40-4:40)

### Scene 5 (2:40-3:20) The inbox

**On screen:** sidebar, tap **Repair bookings**. Show the three groups: **New**, **Booked**, **Done**.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "Customers ask for a repair on the website. They give their device, the fault, and a good time for them. We then call them back. You also get an email for each new booking. They all come here, in three groups: New, Booked, and Done."

### Scene 6 (3:20-4:10) Call the customer and book them

**On screen:** open the test booking. Show device, fault, preferred time. Tap the phone number: the phone starts a call. Come back, write a note ("Screen £X, booked Sat 11am"), set the status to **Booked**, save.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "Open a new booking. Read the device, the fault, and when they want to come in. Tap the phone number and your phone calls them. Agree a price and a time. Then write a short note, like the price and the day, and move it to Booked. Only staff can see the notes. The customer never sees them."

### Scene 7 (4:10-4:40) Mark it done

**On screen:** set the booking to **Done**. Show that it can be moved back to **Booked** if needed.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Say:** "When the repair is finished and the customer has their phone, move it to Done. If you moved it by mistake, just move it back."

### Scene 8 (4:40-5:00) Wrap up

**Say:** "Check both lists every day. Approve good businesses in one tap. Always give a reason when you reject. Call repair customers back the same day if you can."

---

## Common mistakes / if something goes wrong

- **Reject won't go through**: you left the reason empty. Write a reason.
- **Approve or Reject is refused**: the application was already approved or rejected. Refresh the page.
- **Approved the wrong business**: tell the lead. There is no "undo" button in the plan for this.
  > ⚠ Verify against the final UI
- **Tap-to-call does nothing on a laptop**: calling works from the phone. Use your phone, or type the number.
- **A customer says they never heard back**: look in New. Check you moved everyone you called to Booked.

## Rules to remember

- Approve is one tap. Rejecting always needs a reason, and the customer gets it by email.
- Trade customers see prices without VAT, clearly labelled "ex VAT".
- Repair bookings go New, then Booked, then Done. Notes are for staff only.
- Never share a customer's phone number or email outside the shop.
