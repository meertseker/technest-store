# Your daily routine and your first week

Shop hours: Mon-Sat 9am-8pm, Sun 11am-5pm. Shop emails go to **hello@technest.co.uk**.
The admin is at **admin.technest.co.uk**. On a phone, the menu button is at the top left.

> ⚠ Verify against the final UI: page names in the menu (Quick Add, Click & Collect, Devices, Settings, Trade applications, Repair bookings, Import products).

## Every day

### Morning (before opening)

1. **Read the 08:00 low-stock email.** It lists products at or near their reorder level. Reorder what you need. No email means nothing is low.
2. **Open the Click & Collect board.** Pack everything in **To pick**, oldest first. Move each one to **Ready**. The customer gets an email with their collection code.
3. **Check new orders** (**Orders** in the menu, and the "new order" emails). For delivery orders: pack them, then mark them as shipped in the order, so the customer gets a "dispatched" email.
   > ⚠ Verify against the final UI (how to mark an order as shipped)
4. **Check Repair bookings (New)** and **Trade applications** from the night.

### During the day

- When a Click & Collect customer comes in: **check the collection code**, hand over the bag, then tap **Collected**. Their card is only charged at that moment.
- Call new repair customers back. Move them to **Booked** with a note.
- Approve or reject trade applications. A rejection always needs a reason.
- New stock in? Add it with **Quick Add**: photo, check the text, price, stock, devices, publish.

### Evening (before closing)

1. Click & Collect board: is anything still in **To pick**? Pack it. Anything old in **Ready**? The customer gets a reminder after 3 days; after 7 days it is cancelled by itself and the card hold is released. Put cancelled items back on the shelf.
2. All delivery orders packed and marked shipped.
3. Every repair customer you called is moved out of **New**.

## First week after launch: checklist

**Day 1**
- [ ] Log in on your phone and on the shop laptop. Find every Tech Nest page in the menu.
- [ ] Check the shop emails arrive at hello@technest.co.uk (new order, new trade application, new repair booking).
- [ ] Load any stock not yet online with **Import products**: download the template from the page, fill it in, upload it. Read the preview. Each row with a problem is listed. Fix them, then tap **Import**. Rows are matched by SKU (your stock code), so importing the same file again updates products instead of making copies.
  > ⚠ Verify against the final UI

**Day 2**
- [ ] Finish the import. Chargers and power products without UKCA or CE are saved as **drafts**: check the box for the mark, set **Safety marking**, then publish. Vapes are refused; they are never sold online.
- [ ] Watch script 1 (Quick Add). Add 5 new products with it. Aim for under a minute each.

**Day 3**
- [ ] Open **Devices**. Check the newest phones are there, with model numbers as aliases.
- [ ] On your 20 best sellers, check **Fits these devices**.
- [ ] Use the photo widget (script 2) on any best seller with a poor photo.

**Day 4**
- [ ] Open **Settings**. Check: free delivery £20, Klarna minimum £30.
- [ ] Raise the reorder level on fast sellers (for example cables, chargers, screen protectors).

**Day 5**
- [ ] Watch script 3. Handle every Click & Collect order with the board: To pick, Ready, check the code, Collected.
- [ ] Look at one order where you tapped Collected: it should show as paid.

**Day 6**
- [ ] Watch script 6. Clear the trade and repair lists.
- [ ] Is the low-stock email useful? Too long or too short? Adjust reorder levels.

**Day 7**
- [ ] Any Click & Collect order in **Ready** for 3 days or more? Call the customer.
- [ ] Write down anything that was slow or confusing, and send it to the lead.

## Rules to remember

- Vapes are never sold online. Chargers and power banks need UKCA or CE before publishing.
- Photos: only the background changes, and the original is always kept.
- Prices are in pounds and include VAT.
- £1 add-on items can't be a delivery order on their own. Click & Collect is fine.
